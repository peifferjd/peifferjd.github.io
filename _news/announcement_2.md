Our [conference paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.14992) presented at ICORR 2025 in Chicago recieved 2nd place in the "Best Student Paper" section! Thanks to Tim Unger and Arash Sal Moslehian my co-first authors for the fun times and help!

This work validates the markerless motion capture technique that our lab at Shirley Ryan AbilityLab against "gold standard" optical motion capture technology in clinical populations. 

Tim had collected this great dataset, and at BioRob 2024 in Heidelberg we discussed applying our markerless apporach to his data. I had planned to stay in Europe for two more weeks to go hiking, but it ended up being rather poor weather, so I went to Lake Lucerne Institute have a fun hackathon with Tim and Arash while it rained. We got to hike one day at the end!
